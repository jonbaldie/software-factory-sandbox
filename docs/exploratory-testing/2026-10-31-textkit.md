## Journeys

### Generate identifiers

The user wants words joined as camelCase or PascalCase, with later word initials capitalized. `camelCase('Hello-World_foo')` returned `helloWorldFoo`. The variation with a supplementary-plane Deseret initial returned lowercase `𐐨` where uppercase `𐐀` was expected in both helpers. The failure recurred in a fresh process. See [issue #147](https://github.com/jonbaldie/software-factory-sandbox/issues/147).

### Count words

The user wants a count of words made from Unicode letters or numbers. `wordCount('well-known phrase')` returned `2`. The variations containing an isolated U+0301 combining mark returned `1` for the mark alone and `3` for `one`, the mark, and `two`. The documented rule implies `0` and `2`. Both failures recurred in a fresh process. See [issue #148](https://github.com/jonbaldie/software-factory-sandbox/issues/148).

### Fit text into a display

The user wants wrapped or truncated text within the documented code-point limits. `wordWrap('The quick brown fox', 10)` returned `The quick\nbrown fox`, and `truncate('The quick brown fox', 12)` returned `The quick…`. With emoji, `wordWrap('😀😀😀 ok', 2)` returned `😀😀\n😀\nok`, and `truncate('😀😀😀 hello', 3)` returned `😀😀…`. Both variations preserve code-point boundaries and meet the stated limits. No defect was confirmed in these cases.
